Playoff bracket

Seeds set by qualifying scores. In each wildcard the higher seed (#3 or #4) picks the course. All playoff matches 8–10 PM @ Z Center. The final's course is chosen by audience vote.

How team handicaps work

Each player's handicap is multiplied by an allowance, going from the lowest handicap on the roster to the highest. Add those up and round to one decimal place: that's your team handicap, and those strokes come off your team's gross score.

Team sizeLowest2nd3rd4th5th6th

Example: four players on 10, 14, 18 and 22 get 10×11% + 14×8% + 18×6% + 22×5% = 4.4 strokes.

Teams with fewer than 4 players show TBD until the roster is complete. No handicap? The officers will estimate one for you.

    How qualifying works

    1. Teams of 4–6. Three play each hole; sub players between holes.
    2. Scramble: everyone hits, pick the best shot, all play from there.
    3. Play up to 2 rounds of Adare Manor · Back Nine. Only your better round counts.
    4. Net = gross − team handicap. Ties go to the lower gross, then the team that posted first.
    5. Top 6 make the playoffs; seeds 1 and 2 skip straight to the semis.
    6. Submit every round on the score form with a photo of the scorecard. The officers update this table.
